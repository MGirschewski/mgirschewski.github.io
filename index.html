What I believe

Adopting AI is not the same as becoming AI-enabled. One adds tools on top of how you work; the other transforms the work itself, creating real impact rather than just more usage.

I drive that transformation: from defining the vision and shaping the operating model to building and deploying AI agents. Always human-first.

Journey

People, then organisation, then technology.

My consulting years took me hands-on into transformations at every stage, and each one taught me something.

  1. Consulting · 2019–2021

    Learning & Training

    Redesigning development frameworks aligned with organisational strategy.

    What I learned: combining new skills with real business use cases and existing expertise is what turns individual learning into organisational impact.

  2. Consulting · 2019–2025

    Change Management

    Leading change and communication workstreams on large technology-enabled and agile transformations.

    What I learned: how to navigate the change curve, why Change Ambassadors are key, and why communication and transparency matter throughout.

  3. Consulting · 2021–2025

    Operating Model & Organisational Health

    Diagnosing organisational effectiveness and designing target operating models across industries and countries.

    What I learned: strategy, technology and people must pursue the same objectives, or strategy never translates into execution.

  4. SaaS scale-up · 2025–today

    Building with AI

    Turning strategy into operating rhythms, running the company-wide performance cadence, and leading an AI transformation workstream, building automations and agents hands-on.

    What I'm learning: AI is different from previous digital transformations. It changes the roles people play, not just the tools they use.

How I work

The principles behind the work.

Human-first

Technology serves people, not the other way round. I design for the people who will live with the change.

Systems over patches

I look for the root cause in how the organisation operates, then fix the system rather than the symptom.

Strategy counts when it's executed

A strategy is only as good as the priorities, rhythms and decisions that carry it into daily work.

Build to understand

I build the automations and agents myself, because you can't redesign work around AI you haven't used.

Honest about impact

I measure what changed, and I'm clear about what I can and can't attribute to the work.
